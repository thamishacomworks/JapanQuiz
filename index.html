    How to Play

    1. Choose your JLPT level

      Select N5, N4, N3, or N2.

    2. Choose your quiz

      Hiragana: choose the Sinhala meaning. Kanji: choose the correct hiragana reading.

    3. Learn before you play

      Open Word List to review an unlocked level. Save difficult words with Save Word.

    4. Pass and level up

      Answer at least 7 of 10 questions correctly to unlock the next game level.

    5. Practice your way

      Try Practice Mistakes or Mixed Review. Practice modes do not unlock game levels.

    6. Choose your timer

      Before a quiz, pick 5, 10, or 15 seconds per question, or Off for no limit. Each question starts fresh. The timer stops when you answer so you can read the explanation. If time runs out, the question is marked incorrect.
